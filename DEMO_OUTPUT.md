# Fictional demo — a local service landing-page blueprint

**Company:** Cedar Cycle Care (**fictional**)  
**Location:** Amman, Jordan  
**Confirmed services:** Bicycle tune-ups and brake adjustments  
**Audience:** Everyday cyclists and commuters  
**Inquiry channel:** Email — address **not provided**

## Proposed page plan

| Order | Section | Suggested message | UX rationale | Gutenberg core blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hero | "Bicycle tune-ups and brake adjustments in Amman." | Makes the offer and service area clear immediately. | Group, Heading, Paragraph |
| 2 | Services | Separate the two confirmed services. | Helps visitors identify relevant help without invented service details. | Group, Columns, Heading, Paragraph |
| 3 | Who it's for | "For everyday riders and commuters." | Establishes audience relevance. | Group, Heading, Paragraph |
| 4 | Inquiry guidance | Describe your bicycle and requested service when contacting the provider. | Lowers uncertainty and clarifies that an inquiry is not a confirmed booking. | Heading, Paragraph, List |
| 5 | Final action | Propose "Ask About an Appointment by Email" — **DRAFT ONLY**. | Keeps a consistent next step without presenting a fake link. | Heading, Paragraph; Button only after verification |

### Responsive and accessibility planning

Use a readable, single-column mobile layout at narrow widths. Test heading order, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, contrast, target size, reflow and screen-reader interpretation in the **implemented website**. Planned patterns alone do not establish WCAG 2.2 AA conformance.

### Publication decision: **NO-GO**

A functional CTA cannot be configured until an authentic email address is provided. Booking and payment functions are beyond the specified core-block-only scope; fabricated reviews or time guarantees must not be published. No live WordPress page or browser test exists for this example.

> **Note:** This is an illustrative text blueprint, not proof of live deployment, customer results, automation, or accessibility certification.
